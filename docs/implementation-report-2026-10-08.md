# Implementation report: one press per checkpoint (Lattice 0.30.0, 2026-10-08)

Shift+Right and Shift+Left skipped ten steps, and two quick presses of them (within 500 ms) played to the next or previous checkpoint. Holding Right or Left already moves fast, so Olivier asked to drop the ten-step skips and give the checkpoint move to a single press, which also removes the timing logic of the double press. The rules are in spec 6.5, 7.2 (the SKIP row), 7.5 (moves list) and 7.6 (bindings, the paragraph "Checkpoint keys"), with 1 and 15; the rationale is in design report 3.12 and decision 40 (decisions 32 and 37 amended). The design was reviewed by Olivier before the work (`claude/v0.30-design.md` in the project): the actions renamed `next-checkpoint` and `prev-checkpoint`, holding the keys moves one checkpoint, nothing happens at the last checkpoint, the presenter names the step each key reaches and its kind, version 0.30.0.

## 1. What was built

- **Bindings** (`model.py`): `next-checkpoint` (Shift+Right) and `prev-checkpoint` (Shift+Left) in place of `skip-forward` and `skip-back`. `key_bindings` merges the defaults with the front matter's `keys:` and maps the old names through `KEY_ALIASES`, so a deck that rebinds `skip-forward` keeps its key; `graph.py` (LT018) and `emit.py` (the deck JSON) both use it, so the JSON only carries the new names.
- **Runtime** (`lattice.js`):
  - `actions["next-checkpoint"]` and `actions["prev-checkpoint"]` play to `nextCheckpoint` and `prevCheckpoint` of an anchor: the target of a playback that the same action started on this slide and that is still under way (`checkpointAnchor`, read before the playback is stopped), else the current step. Two quick presses therefore go two checkpoints ahead, with no timing rule; the other direction counts from where the slide is.
  - `onKey` ignores `KeyboardEvent.repeat` for these two actions only, so holding them moves one checkpoint; Right and Left keep their auto-repeat.
  - Removed: the ten-step actions, `BURST`, `BURST_WINDOW_MS`, `burst`, `burstMove` and its calls, the `hold` and `checkpoint` options of `playSteps` (its `playing` record now names the action that started it). About 50 lines out, 25 in.
  - Presenter: a moves line per key, "Shift+→ checkpoint: step K of S (KIND)", absent when the key would not move, or "stopped by a blocking detour step" when one lies before the checkpoint. `stepKind` names the kind of the step reached (spec 6.5) by the types of the tracks that change on arrival, followers left out: `reveal`, `columns` or the component name (`bbv-anim`, `arrow`), joined with ` + `; step 0 and silent steps have none. The Keybindings section labels the two actions and loses the "×2" row.
- **Docs**: spec (above); README (the Navigation table); `docs/USER_SKILL.md` (the keys and checkpoints rows, detour steps, the snapshot timing note, the "Rules that bite" line on combined cues); `docs/SKILL.md` (the runtime line, the `test_runtime.py` row); design report (3.12, decisions 32, 37 and 40, roadmap v0.30); todo (the 0.27.0 item on the window replaced by a 0.30.0 item, the done 0.29.0 git item removed); the manual's `presenting-keys`, `checkpoints` (text and speaker notes), `navigation-details` and timelines slides; the 0.29 report copied to `docs/archive/` and listed in its README (to `git rm` in `docs/`); version 0.30.0.
