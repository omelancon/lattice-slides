# Implementation report: Left walks the slides, Backspace walks the history (Lattice 0.29.0, 2026-10-07)

Left used to do two jobs: step back on the slide, then pop the history, and only with an empty history walk the structure. Backspace was a second key for Up, and PageUp and PageDown duplicated Left and Right. Olivier asked for a clear split between moving through the slides and moving through the history: Left only moves to the previous step or slide, Backspace undoes the last move, Up alone returns from a detour, and PageUp and PageDown go. The rules are in spec 7.1, 7.2 (PREV, the new UNDO, the consequences), 7.3 (the worked trace, extended), 7.5 (moves list) and 7.6 (bindings), with 15; the rationale is in design report 3.2, 3.12 and decision 39 (decisions 5 and 15 amended). The design was reviewed by Olivier before the work (`claude/v0.29-design.md` in the project): Left records its moves, Backspace leaves a slide at once, Up is not undoable, Left out of a detour's first slide closes the detour, "Nothing to undo", the two new lines in the presenter's moves, the name `undo`.

## 1. What was built

- **Bindings** (`model.py`, `DEFAULT_KEYS`): `next` is Right and Space, `prev` Left, the new `undo` Backspace, `return` Up. `undo` is listed before `return`, so a deck that binds `return: [ArrowUp, Backspace]` keeps Backspace on `return` (the later action wins in `keyMap`, as for `first-step` and `home` in 0.27). PageUp and PageDown are free, so they may now be slide keys (LT018 reads `DEFAULT_KEYS`).
- **Runtime** (`lattice.js`):
  - `prevMove()` says what PREV does without doing it: the previous step (skipping detour steps, as before), else the structural predecessor at its last step, else nothing. When the slide is the entry of a detour, its predecessor is the origin, and the topmost excursion of the history was started on that origin, the move closes that excursion and lands where RETURN would (the step the excursion recorded). The action and the presenter's moves list share it.
  - `actions.prev` never pops the history. A slide change pushes a `backward` entry; when it closes a detour, the entry keeps the entries it removed (`closed`) and the transition is RETURN's `zoom-out`. "No previous slide" at the start, as before.
  - `actions.undo` pops the last entry and goes to its slide and step from any step of the current slide, putting back the `closed` entries of a `backward` one. The transition is the reverse of the move it takes back (`undoHow`): the backward `next` transition for a forward entry, `zoom-out` for an excursion, the forward `next` transition for a Left, `zoom-in` for a Left that had closed a detour. With an empty history it flashes "Nothing to undo".
  - Presenter: the moves list gains a line for Left (the step it goes to, the slide, or "leave the detour, to ORIGIN") and one for Backspace ("undo: back to LABEL", when the history is not empty), each with the deck's first key for the action; the Keybindings section labels `prev` "previous step or slide" and `undo` "undo the last move (history)"; Backspace is shown as `⌫`.
- **Docs**: spec (above); README (the Navigation table); `docs/USER_SKILL.md` (the excursion term, the keys and history rows); `docs/SKILL.md` (the invariant "Structure and history are separate keys", the `test_runtime.py`, `test_graph.py` and runtime lines); design report (3.2, 3.12, decisions 5, 15 and 39, roadmap v0.29); todo; the manual's "Presenting: the keys" (`presenting-keys`, a Backspace row, its rebinding line shortened to keep the slide inside its body), "Left, Backspace and Up: structure and history" (`history`, rewritten), and the mentions of Backspace and Left in "Links and notes", the detour demo and "Where the option landed"; the 0.28 report copied to `docs/archive/` and listed in its README (to `git rm` in `docs/`, see the todo); version 0.29.0.
